STAR Validation’s 94.8% is face validity, how strongly users endorse their own profile. The incumbents are judged here on test-retest reliability and predictive validity. They are different constructs, and this page keeps them apart. The 94.8%, the derived NPS of +100 and the promoter share all belong to the external study of nine ratings, as of 28 June 2026. DiSC

Internal consistency .80–.86; weak predictive validity [7, 8]

Respectable internal consistency, but primarily a behavioural observation tool. Little evidence it predicts success in specific roles. It captures how a person acts, not why.

Insights Discovery

British Psychological Society: 1.5 / 5 [9]

The four colour-energies model, heavily marketed across the UK and Europe. Independent BPS assessment awards it just 1.5 stars out of 5 for accuracy and validity.

Enneagram

Systematic review (104 samples): mixed evidence [5, 6]

Popular in coaching and corporate retreats. A 2021 review of 104 samples found only mixed reliability and validity, with little support for wings or inter-type movement. High appeal, low rigour.

MBTI

~50% change type after five weeks [3, 4]

Taken by millions annually, yet roughly half of people are assigned a different type when retested just five weeks later. Forces traits into binary categories; predictive power for performance is practically zero.

STAR Validation

94.8% user-endorsed accuracy (n=9), +100 derived NPS

Built on the STAR Cognitive Algorithm (CA2.4), synthesising seven psychological theories into four types and twelve archetypes. Early face validity is exceptional; predictive validity is the next milestone to prove.

03 · User ratings

Every external rating landed between 87% and 100%.

Nine genuine external users rated the accuracy of their Cognitive Profile Report. The spread is tight, with a population standard deviation of 4.3 points, and two perfect scores. Teal bars mark the 100% ratings. All respondents are anonymised. The sum of the nine ratings is 853, so the mean is 853 ÷ 9 = 94.8% to one decimal place. These workings use only the nine scores published with the 28 June 2026 study.

04 · Derived NPS

A Net Promoter Score of +100.

Applying the agreed banding, below 85% scores 8/10, 85–95% scores 9/10, above 95% scores 10/10, every single rating becomes a promoter. With zero passives and zero detractors across the sample, the derived NPS hits the statistical ceiling. That is (9 − 0) ÷ 9, expressed as a percentage: +100. This is the external study as of 28 June 2026. It is a separate figure from the +95 NPS score published on STAR Assess.

05 · The implication
“
The MBTI flips a coin on your personality every five weeks. Insights Discovery holds a 1.5-out-of-5 from the BPS. Against that backdrop, a tool returning 94.8% endorsed accuracy and a +100 NPS is not merely competitive; it is disruptive.

The sentence above describes the external study as of 28 June 2026. The competitor clauses are the claims already cited on this page, at [3], [4] and [9].

The hook

Users agree with their profiles at 94.8%. That face validity is the wedge into a market the incumbents hold by inertia, not by evidence.

The next milestone

Convert that endorsement into predictive validity: proving the four types and twelve archetypes forecast team cohesion, leadership and commercial outcomes.
